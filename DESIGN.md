## Brand & Style

This design system fuses the raw, unapologetic energy of subcultural punk with the exacting restraint of an haute-couture editorial publication. The core visual language embraces "Dark Elegance" and "Edgy Minimalism"—defined by stark black-and-white contrasts, disciplined line work, expansive atmospheric whitespace, and deliberate electric shocks of soft punk pink. 

The aesthetic targets discerning fashion rebels and cultural connoisseurs who demand uncompromising luxury without sterile conformity. Interface elements mirror gallery framing and collectible print magazines: razor-sharp edges, deliberate typographical tension between delicate serifs and utilitarian grotesque geometry, and tactile architectural lines that bisect the canvas.

## Colors

The palette relies on strict, purposeful distribution rather than equal weight. 

- **Canvas & Elevation**: The primary background is canvas off-white (`#F9F9F9`), preventing clinical glare while maintaining a clean, gallery-like foundation. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved exclusively for elevated card surfaces, layered drawers, and distinct modular containers.
- **Structural Jet Black (`#111111`)**: Drives the primary typographic hierarchy, ultra-thin rule dividers, crisp framing borders, and inverted high-contrast elements.
- **Electric Soft Pink (`#FF4D8D`)**: The hero accent. Used deliberately for focal interactive drivers, critical action buttons, active tags, and strategic hover strikes. It must never coat entire backgrounds; it exists to punctuate.
- **Subtle Deep Burgundy (`#4A0E17`)**: Serves as the brooding, darkened counterpart to pink. Applied to hover states, focused input rims, secondary selections, and press interactions, creating depth without losing the gothic undertone.
- **Text Hierarchy**: Titles and primary labels use `#111111`. Secondary body copy defaults to `#555555` to balance contrast against the off-white ground.

## Typography

Typography delivers dramatic tension:
- **Headings (Bodoni Moda)**: High-contrast, editorial serif that echoes iconic fashion broadsheets and zine mastheads. Display weights carry negative letter-spacing for tight, architectural lockups.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, neutral sans-serif designed for contemporary clarity. It prevents visual fatigue when reading editorial essays or product specifications.
- **Micro-labels & CTAs**: Transformed into all-caps (`text-transform: uppercase`) paired with generous letter spacing (`0.14em` to `0.18em`) to reinforce precision and structural containment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, translating to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Outer Margins**: Substantial canvas margins (`5rem` on desktop) enforce an expansive, high-fashion breathing room that avoids edge clutter.
- **Rhythm & Rules**: Structural containment is achieved using visible 1px hairline rules (`#111111` or `#E5E5E5`) aligned to column gutters rather than invisible breaks. 
- **Adaptation Rules**: Multi-column editorial modules collapse sequentially into full-width monolithic stacks on mobile viewports. On viewports below 768px, inner component padding shifts down by one scale tier to preserve critical horizontal readability.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft diffuse blurs, drop shadows, and skeuomorphic gradients in favor of sharp planar depth and stark tonal separation:

- **Borders & Rules**: Depth is created strictly through 1px crisp borders (`#111111` for high-priority outlines, `#E5E5E5` for secondary division).
- **Surface Elevation**: Ground level rests at `#F9F9F9`. Raised cards, dialogue modals, and slide-over panels use solid `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px `#111111` contour.
- **Offset Pop Overlays**: Flyouts and key interactive badges utilize an unblurred, hard-edged offset border effect (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #111111`) rather than a soft shadow, maintaining the aggressive punk foundation.
- **Modals & Drawers**: Modals drop with a stark scrim overlay using `#111111` at 70% opacity, locking visual focus to the floating pure white panel.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

No rounded corners, soft bubbles, or pill-shaped containers are permitted. Buttons, input fields, badges, cards, imagery viewports, and modals must terminate in razor-sharp 90-degree angles. This architectural austerity reinforces the edgy, unyielding nature of the brand while emphasizing editorial refinement.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#FF4D8D` background, sharp 0px corners, 1px `#111111` border, text set in uppercase `label-lg` with `#111111` font color. Hover state shifts instantly to `#4A0E17` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or transparent background, 1px `#111111` border, `#111111` text. Hover state inverts background to `#111111` and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text-Only Link**: Uppercase `label-md` with an active 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline; underline shifts to `#FF4D8D` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- 0px border radius, 1px `#111111` border, `#FFFFFF` background, uppercase `label-md` text.
- Active or Selected chip: Background changes to `#111111` with `#FFFFFF` text and a subtle 2px accent pip colored `#FF4D8D` on the left.

### Form Inputs
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` fill with a crisp 1px `#111111` outline.
- Labels: Placed above fields in uppercase `label-md` (`#111111`).
- Focus State: The 1px outline expands to 2px solid `#FF4D8D` with zero diffuse glow.
- Placeholder: `#888888` in `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: A 16x16px square, 0px border radius, 1px `#111111` border. When checked, the box fills solid `#111111` with a `#FF4D8D` inverted check icon.
- Radio: A 16x16px diamond or square frame containing a centered 6x6px solid `#FF4D8D` square when selected.

### Cards & Editorial Panels
- Flat `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px `#111111` hairline frame.
- Image containers within cards preserve an edge-to-edge crop with 0px corner radiuses.
- Card footers feature a dedicated 1px horizontal separator line partitioning metadata from media.

### Lookbook / Editorial Carousel Indicator
- Minimalist linear tick indicators (horizontal dashes, 16px wide by 2px tall) in `#111111`, transitioning to `#FF4D8D` for active slide identification.
